# 🏠 Lunar Habitat Design (SIFI Base)
**Building a Permanent Human Settlement Using Lunar Resources**

## 📌 Concept Overview
The SIFI Base is designed to be a self-sustaining habitat built entirely from materials extracted and processed by the Lunar Pyramid ISRU facility. We eliminate the need to import heavy building materials from Earth.

## 🧱 Construction Materials
1. **Lunar Glass:** Produced by melting regolith in our solar furnace. Used for windows, greenhouse domes, and radiation shielding.
2. **Regolith Blocks:** Sintered (heated) regolith blocks used for structural walls. They provide excellent thermal mass and radiation protection.
3. **Metals (Iron & Titanium):** Extracted from the regolith and used for internal framing, airlock doors, and piping.

## 🏗️ Construction Method: 3D Printing & Lava Tubes
We utilize a dual-approach for habitat construction:
*   **Phase 1: Surface Modules (The Greenhouse):** Using a robotic 3D printer, we build dome structures using sulfur-regolith concrete. These serve as initial laboratories and greenhouses.
*   **Phase 2: Subsurface Habitats (Lava Tubes):** We utilize natural lunar lava tubes (underground tunnels) to protect astronauts from radiation and extreme temperatures. We seal the entrances with our Lunar Glass airlocks.

## 🛡️ QHSE & Safety Features (30 Years of Expertise)
*   **Radiation Shielding:** Walls are built with a minimum thickness of 2 meters of regolith.
*   **Dust Mitigation:** All airlocks are equipped with UV-C neutralization chambers and our patented "Puzzle Grounding Mat" to ensure zero dust enters the living quarters.
*   **Emergency Life Support:** The habitat is connected directly to the water and oxygen production lines of the SIFI H2 facility.

## 🔄 Circular Economy
*   Water is recycled with 98% efficiency.
*   Oxygen is supplied from the electrolysis of lunar water.
*   Food is grown in the Lunar Glass greenhouses using hydroponics.
